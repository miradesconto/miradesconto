Planning a gaming setup: a practical checklist

A balanced setup makes a bigger difference than buying the most expensive component in each category. Start with the device you already own.

← All English guides

1. Decide what will run your games

A console has a defined performance target, while a PC's results depend on its graphics card, CPU, memory and game settings. Before purchasing a monitor or television, verify the gaming platform's available output resolutions and refresh rates. Product marketing numbers do not necessarily reflect every game's frame rate.

2. Choose your display around real use

For a desk, screen size, distance and clarity usually matter more than a very large diagonal. For a sofa, a TV with low input lag and suitable HDR performance can be more practical. If a monitor advertises 180 Hz, confirm whether your exact PC, cable and port support that mode at your chosen resolution. Console games may have lower limits even when the screen supports more.

Look for measured response times, variable refresh rate compatibility and sensible HDR brightness instead of relying only on packaging badges.

3. Audio, capture and desk space

Audio routing is different for direct console play and streaming through a computer. A capture card generally brings game video and audio into broadcasting software, while party chat, voice monitoring and microphone input can require additional routing. Check the card's capture and passthrough resolutions independently.

  • Choose a comfortable, reliably connected headset or microphone.
  • Plan monitor or laptop arms only after checking weight and VESA dimensions.
  • Keep power cables and signal cables organized and away from movement.
  • Prioritize ergonomics, a stable desk and lighting before decorative extras.

4. Build in phases

First buy what solves an immediate problem: a correct cable, a sturdy mount or a suitable microphone. Upgrade the display or processing hardware only when you can identify the bottleneck. Check model compatibility, warranty, actual final price and retailer reputation before buying.
