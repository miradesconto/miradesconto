How MiraDesconto works

Observed prices

Where possible, we compare observations of the same listing over time. A store's crossed-out price does not prove a historic low.

Independent guidance

Our guides explain specifications and trade-offs. We do not claim firsthand tests unless explicitly documented.

Affiliate transparency

Eligible purchases may earn us a commission without an additional affiliate fee to you. The retailer manages checkout and delivery.
