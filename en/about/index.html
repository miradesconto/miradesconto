About our project

Technology information, with fewer distractions.

MiraDesconto brings technology offers, observed prices and independent buying information into one place. We focus on computers, phones, monitors, gaming gear and practical accessories sold in Brazil.

We are not a store

Products are offered by third-party retailers. Checkout, payment, shipping, warranty and customer support are handled by the seller. You should always verify final conditions on the seller's website.

What our price radar means

When enough data is available, our radar compares the same listing against prices recorded over time. A crossed-out reference price in an ad does not establish a historic low. Data collection can be delayed or interrupted, so a displayed price is an observation, not a guarantee that the offer remains available.

Affiliate disclosure

Some outgoing retailer links may be affiliate links. MiraDesconto may earn a commission on eligible purchases; that relationship does not add a separate affiliate fee to the buyer. We do not claim to have personally tested a product unless we explicitly document such testing.

What is available in English?

Our English pages offer recently tracked listings and a growing collection of original buying guides. The broader article archive, some price methodology details and most product pages are in Brazilian Portuguese. Product names and prices may appear in Portuguese and BRL because they come from local retailer listings.
